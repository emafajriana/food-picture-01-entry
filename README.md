# Rijstafel Feast — Indonesian Banquet Overhead

Entry for the make.fun bounty **food-picture-01** ("Make the best-looking food picture").

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) validates the bundled
picture against the competition limits (whole PNG or JPEG, at most
1 MiB, at most 1024x1024) and copies it byte for byte to
`entry.png` in the output directory (`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set,
otherwise `/output`).

The picture: a vibrant overhead Indonesian rijstafel feast on a banana
leaf — a golden yellow rice dome at the centre topped with crispy
shallots and herbs, surrounded by rich beef rendang, grilled chicken
sate skewers with peanut sauce, fried tempeh, red and green sambals,
pickles, and colourful kerupuk crackers. Warm, abundant, and made to
look irresistible at first glance, no text.
